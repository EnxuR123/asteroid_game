**Asteroids-Style Space Shooter**

For this game we replicated the famous asteroid shooting game.

In the game it features:
  - acceleration of the space ship.
  - Drag of the space ship.
  - The space ship looping around the edge of the screens
  - Asteroids floating around that will split in half if being hit by the player bullet.
  - Asteroids that will respawn in waves
  - An enemy space ship that will chase after the player and shoot at him using dot product.
  - If the player hits the asteroids, the enemy space ship, or the enemy bullet the player loses.
